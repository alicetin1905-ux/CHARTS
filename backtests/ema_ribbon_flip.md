# EMA Ribbon [Krypt] flip — BTCUSDT perpetual backtest

Generated by `scripts/ribbon_backtest.py`. Candles: OKX BTC-USDT-SWAP (Bybit's API is geo-blocked here; the two
perps trade within a few dollars). 1× leverage, full equity per trade, 0.075% cost per side (Bybit taker 0.055%
+ slippage). Signals on the candle close, filled at the next open.

Rules: long when all 8 EMAs (20/25/30/35/40/45/50/55) flip into bullish order, short on the bearish flip; the
opposite flip reverses the position.

- **plain**: the flip only
- **stop**: flip + 2.5 × ATR(14) stop-loss
- **squeeze**: flip + stop, only if the ribbon was squeezed to one line (width ≤ 0.5 × ATR) in the last 10 candles
- **cross**: EMA 20 × EMA 55 cross + stop, instead of the full 8-EMA flip
