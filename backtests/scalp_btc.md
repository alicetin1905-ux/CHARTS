## Rules

| Step | LONG (SHORT mirrored) |
|---|---|
| HTF filter (1H / 4H) | last closed candle: close > EMA 200 and EMA 50 > EMA 200 |
| Setup (15m / 30m / 1H) | last closed candle: Chandelier Exit (22, 3) long and close > ZLSMA 32 |
| Entry (3m / 5m / 15m) | closed green candle with LONG score ≥ LONG min score; market order at its close |
| SL | SL % from entry |
| TP1 → TP2 → TP3 | close 40 / 35 / 25 % of the original size; each only out of what is still open |
| Break-even | after TP1 the stop moves to entry + 0.2 % (covers fees); after TP2 to TP1 |
| ZLSMA exit | an entry-TF candle closes below ZLSMA 32: the rest of the position closes at market. Tested three ways: always, only after TP1, off (×3 configurations) |

**LONG score** (0–100; the SHORT score uses the mirrored checks):

| Part | Check on the entry candle | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 30m trend bullish | last closed 30m candle: close > EMA 50 and EMA 20 > EMA 50 | 10 |
| 1H trend bullish | same on 1H | 10 |
| Resistance breakout | close > the highest high of the 20 candles before | 15 |
| Volume expansion | volume ≥ multiplier × the average of the 20 candles before | 10 |
| ATR expansion | ATR 14 ≥ expansion × its 20-candle average | 10 |
| Order-book imbalance | **no history exists — left out** | (10) |
| Aggressive market buys | taker-buy volume ≥ 55 % of the candle's volume (≤ 45 % for SHORT) | 10 |
| Momentum bullish | MACD 12/26/9 line > signal and RSI 14 > 50 | 15 |
| Liquidity sweep / reclaim | a low in the last 5 candles broke the 20-candle low before them; close back above it | 10 |

Score = points / 90 × 100. Costs: 0.055 % taker + 0.02 % slippage on entries, stops and ZLSMA exits (stops fill at the
open when price gaps through); 0.02 % maker on targets, filled on a touch. Replayed on 1m candles: stop first, then targets,
then the ZLSMA check at the candle close. One position at a time. **1R = the SL distance**: at 1 % risk per trade, +1R = +1 %
of the account (no compounding).
