# BTCUSDT.P compression → expansion scalper

Generated by `scripts/scalp_squeeze.py`. Binance USDⓈ-M BTCUSDT 1m candles, **train Jan 2023 – Dec 2024, test Jan 2025 – Sep 2026**.

## Rules (the same for LONG and SHORT, mirrored; every threshold has its own LONG and SHORT value)

| Step | Rule (LONG; SHORT mirrored) | Tested values |
|---|---|---|
| Compression (setup TF) | Bollinger Band width (20, 2) in the lowest p % of its last 100 candles, now or in the 2 candles before | setup 15m / 30m / 1H · p 10 / 20 / 30 % |
| Box | high / low of the last N closed setup candles | N 10 / 20 |
| Expansion (entry TF) | the first entry candle to close above the box high, green | entry 3m / 5m / 15m (smaller than setup) |
| — volume | volume ≥ v × average of the 20 candles before | v 1.5 / 2 / 3 |
| — range | true range ≥ k × ATR 14 | k 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 |
| — flow | taker buys ≥ 55 % of the candle's volume (SHORT: taker sells) | off / on |
| HTF filter | 1H or 4H: close > EMA 200 and EMA 50 > EMA 200 | off / 1H / 4H |
| Exits | SL; TP1 / TP2 / TP3 close 40 / 35 / 25 % (each only out of what is still open); after TP1 stop to entry + 0.2 %, after TP2 to TP1; ZLSMA 32 exit on an entry candle close | SL 0.5 / 0.7 / 1 % · 16 TP ladders · ZLSMA after TP1 / off |

**248,832 rule sets per side**, each backtested LONG-only and SHORT-only. Market entries at the candle close (0.055 % taker
+ 0.02 % slippage), stops taker + slippage (at the open when price gaps through), targets 0.02 % maker on a touch; replayed on 1m
candles, stop first. 1R = the SL distance (= 1 % of the account at 1 % risk). One position at a time.
