# Delta Volume indicator: price / CVD divergence BUY / SELL signals, 6 years

Generated by `scripts/cvd_divergence_backtest.py`. Binance USDⓈ-M BTCUSDT 1m candles, **2020-10-01 – 2026-09-30**. Signals from [`indicators/delta_volume.pine`](../indicators/delta_volume.pine) at its default settings (pivots 5 / 3, 5–60 bars apart, 10 bars to confirm, confirmation candle needs matching delta, no hidden divergences, no CVD reset).

- Entry at the next bar's open, 1×, full equity, 0.075 % cost per side. Stop at the divergence pivot, checked on 1m candles. An opposite signal closes and reverses; a repeat signal is ignored.
- **reverse**: no target. **2R**: take profit at twice the stop distance.
- Delta: **intrabar** = the indicator's 1m intrabar estimate (5m on 1D), as TradingView computes it live; **chart bar** = the fallback the indicator uses on bars older than TradingView's intrabar history; **taker** = Binance's real taker buy − sell volume (not available in Pine).
- Max drawdown on daily closes, open trades marked to market. Returns compound.
- Buy & hold over the same period: **+676%**.
