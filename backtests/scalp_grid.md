## Rules

- **Score** = TradeBot's ATLAS score (`src/atlasScore.js`, classic mode) with the inputs a backtest has: price, volume and the
  daily pivot. The order-flow inputs of the live bot (funding, open interest, long/short ratio, order book, taker tape) cannot be
  replayed. The Python port (`scripts/atlas_score.py`) gives the same score as the bot's own code on 645 of 645 sampled
  15m / 1H / 4H candles (`scripts/check_score.js`).
- **Setup**: the last closed setup-TF candle scores ≥ +min score (long) or ≤ −min score (short).
- **HTF filter**: the last closed HTF candle's score leans the same way (≥ +25 / ≤ −25, ATLAS's bias threshold).
- **Entry**: a closed entry-TF candle in the trade's direction (green for longs, red for shorts) with volume ≥ multiplier × the
  average of the 20 candles before it, and ATR(14) ≥ expansion × its own 20-candle average. Market order at that close.
- **Exits**: stop at SL %; TP1 / TP2 / TP3 close 40 / 35 / 25 % (TradeBot's split). After TP1 the stop goes to entry + 0.2 %,
  after TP2 to TP1 (TradeBot's live rules). Replayed on 1m candles, stop first when a minute touches both; no time limit.
- **Costs**: 0.055 % taker + 0.02 % slippage on the entry and on stops (filled at the open when price gaps through);
  0.02 % maker on targets (filled on a touch). One position per coin at a time.
- **R**: 1R = the SL distance. At 1 % risk per trade, +1R = +1 % of the account (6 coins, fixed risk, no compounding).
